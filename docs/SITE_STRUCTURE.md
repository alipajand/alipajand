# Site structure

- **`/` (home)**: the quick read. Hero and positioning, two selected case studies, about,
  capabilities, open-source tools, recent writing, contact.
- **`/portfolio`**: the case-study index. Each dedicated case study has its own page at
  `/portfolio/[slug]` with the problem, role, decisions, evidence and outcome.
- **`/open-source`**: the AI and developer-experience tools, with the GitHub contribution calendar.
- **`/writing`**: articles on frontend architecture, design systems, AI-assisted development and DX.
- **`/engineering-principles`**: the operating model, with links to the case studies and posts that
  show each principle.
- **`/now`**: current focus, dated by hand.

Use home for first impressions and contact, and the portfolio and case studies for evidence and
narrative length. The header links Portfolio, Open Source, Writing, Principles and Contact; `/now` is
reachable from the sitemap and by URL only.
