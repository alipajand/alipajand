# 05 · Review results

`capture/review.mjs` runs every check below in headless Chromium, with a short WebKit pass at the end. `capture/journey.mjs` walks the navigation between both prototypes. The latest runs are in [`capture/review-results.json`](capture/review-results.json) and below.

**Result (P2.2, fresh run): 103 pass, 0 fail, 1 skip.** The skip is Firefox, which is not installed in this environment. No Firefox result is claimed. The P2.1 run, for comparison, was 84 pass, 0 fail, 1 skip; the suite grew by the checks for the opening's stable layout, the return anchors, the bays' link order, and the phone figures and label sizes.

**Navigation journey (fresh run): all checks pass** at 1440 × 900, at 390 × 844 and under reduced motion. See "Navigation" below.

These are automated checks in a headless engine. They are not a screen-reader test, a real-device test or a shipping-Safari test (see 04). **Passing them does not make the creative work finished** (see 06).

## Navigation

`node capture/journey.mjs` (with `W=390 H=844` for a phone and `RM=1` for reduced motion). Fresh run, all three configurations pass:

- All 30 same-site links on the five pages (P2 index, AG, EM; P1 LedgerGuard scene and case study) return 200, and every `#anchor` they name exists on the target page.
- The only links to alipajand.com are the four supporting and experiment case studies on the index and one LedgerGuard article on its case study. No featured project is sent to production.
- **Index → LedgerGuard case study → "Back to the work"** lands on LedgerGuard in the condensed register (bay top 102 px on desktop, 212 px on a phone). Browser **Back** returns to the case study; **Forward** returns to LedgerGuard in the register.
- **Index → LedgerGuard Scene**: the P1 logo goes to the register; "Work" goes to and lands on LedgerGuard; Back returns to the scene.
- **Cross-project:** LedgerGuard case → "Next sheet" → AlwaysGeeky case (`#case` at the top) → next → Emplifi case. The sheet navigation reads previous / "All work" (`index.html#project-<slug>`) / next on both. Emplifi's "All work" lands on Emplifi. Back, Back returns to Emplifi then AlwaysGeeky; Forward, Forward returns to Emplifi in the register.
- AlwaysGeeky's bar "Index" lands on AlwaysGeeky; its bay's Scene chip opens the scene. Both LedgerGuard pages send AlwaysGeeky and Emplifi to the P2 sheets.
- Layout shift on every landing: 0.
- No page errors or failed requests. (The reduced-motion run first found an unhandled "Transition was skipped" rejection on every page change: skipping a view transition rejects its `ready` promise. All four skip sites now catch it.)

## Defects found and fixed in P2.2

Found while recomposing and reviewing, not by the earlier suite:

1. **The opening shifted the page.** The P2.1 condensation animated a margin and two heights (CLS 0.117 desktop, 0.378 phone). Rebuilt on a fixed-height stage with transforms and clipping (see 02 and 04).
2. **LedgerGuard's logo, "Work" and "Back to the work" went to the P1 index**, whose "other sheets" list sent AlwaysGeeky and Emplifi to the production site. All now return into this register at LedgerGuard; the list and a new "Next sheet" go to the P2 sheets.
3. **Return links landed at the top of the index**, above the opening. They now land on the project's bay in the condensed register.
4. **The bay's whole picture was the Scene link** and came before the case study in the reading order. The picture now repeats the case-study link (out of the tab order), and Scene is a small secondary chip on the picture's corner, after it in the tab order.
5. **Phones showed every drawing twice**, once in the stage window too small to read and once in a sideways-scrolling frame. The window is gone; each step has one phone sheet.
6. **Phone labels under 12 px** at 360 px (step codes 11.3 px, the EM-01 host labels 10.9 px). Set larger; the review now measures them.
7. **AG-02's "Frontend conventions" line wrapped out of its box** after the type change. The line wraps later.
8. **Unhandled view-transition rejection under reduced motion** (see Navigation).
9. **"The interface," sat on the cut line** in the live first frame on wide screens. The datum was lifted above the cut with the CSS `translate` property, which GSAP clears when it animates the element's transform. It is now hung from its foot with `bottom`, and the review checks that the two datums sit either side of the cut.

## Defects found and fixed in P2.1 (historical)

Found in stills and recorded frames, not by the automated checks:

1. **EM-02 station labels overlapped their sub-labels** ("Input to the module" wrapped into "Data or interaction"). The boxes are taller and the labels wrap earlier.
2. **The AG chain faded while its card was still on screen.** The match cut began at the landing beat's first frame. Both scenes now hold their last drawing for the first 12% of the landing span and fade it by 24%, so the card is mostly read first.
3. **The surface drop lines dangled at the top of the frame** after the AG camera moved down. They now fade with the surfaces.
4. **Cut-band tags were truncated** ("Shipped screensh…"). The tags are shorter, the split favours the material above the cut, and below 1,360 px only the sheet code shows; the brief names the material in full.
5. **The left A bubble covered the "The interface," datum**, and the dash-dot line ran through the datum text. The datums now sit clear of the bubble, wholly on their own side of the cut.
6. **In the condensed row, the cut ran through the key column's text.** The key-column end of the cut and the left bubble fade with the datums.
7. **AlwaysGeeky's long name pushed "Case study →" onto its own line**, so its title block did not match the other two. Every bay now puts the cue under the name; on phones each name reserves two lines so the three cues align.
8. **A brief lowercased "React" and "D3".** Fixed at the source.
