### The project interaction

The boundary step now shows the rule where it lives in the product, using LedgerGuard's own design prototype instead of a drawn demonstration.

- **Scrolling** plays the workspace's review states over the rest of the step: Ready for review, Partially reviewed, Confirmed. Each change is a 240 ms cross-fade between captures of the prototype; the reading text does not move.
- **Choosing a state** by hand: three buttons in the card (`aria-pressed`) switch every view of the workspace, keep focus, and announce, for example, "Showing partially reviewed: 6 of 13 confirmed." through a polite live region. Scrolling into the next state takes over again.
- **Opening the prototype**: "Open the workspace prototype" goes to `workspace.html`, a local copy of the interactive prototype (page design v1.0.1). There the reader can review next term, confirm, edit a value, and switch role and scenario. A bar at the top says "Design prototype · fictional data · not the shipped product" and links back to `index.html#layer-boundary`.
- **Provenance** is stated in the card: the shipped behaviour the prototype is drawn on (statuses, actions, the notice-deadline rule), and what is proposed and not built (a separate Corrected chip, tabs, viewer zoom, moving to the next term after a confirm). Both come from the prototype's design notes.

On phones, and with motion off, the card carries the workspace image itself, so the state change is visible while reading. On wide screens with motion on, the stage shows it beside the card.

See [03-boundary-interaction.mp4](recordings/03-boundary-interaction.mp4).

### Navigation contract

| Need                          | How                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Skip the sequence             | First Tab stop: "Skip the sequence" → `#project-ledgerguard`. The bar also has **Skip intro**.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Reach the work                | Bar **Work** → `#work`. The layer index (bottom left) jumps to any sheet, with `aria-current="step"` on the sheet in view.                                                                                                                                               |
| Reach contact                 | Bar **Contact** → `#contact`, always visible.                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Open the case study           | "Read the LedgerGuard case study" in the story.                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Return without losing context | Browser **Back** restores the exact scroll position (measured: 10243 → 10243) and skips the arrival animation. The case study's **Back to the work** link goes to `index.html#project-ledgerguard`, and the story heading turns red as the `:target` "you are here" cue. |
| Turn motion off               | Bar **Motion on/off** toggle (`aria-pressed`), remembered per browser. It follows `prefers-reduced-motion` until the visitor chooses.                                                                                                                                    |
